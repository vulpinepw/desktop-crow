Desktop Crow 1.4.0 for Windows 10/11 (x64)
Made by tordev.

Install
-------
1. Run Desktop-Crow-Setup-1.4.0.exe. It installs for your user only (no
   administrator rights needed), with a Start-menu entry and a desktop
   shortcut.
2. The installer is not code-signed, so SmartScreen may say "Windows
   protected your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway".
3. Start Desktop Crow from the Start menu. On first launch it asks for your
   crow's name and whether it is a she or a he; then the crow flies in. Its
   icon sits in the notification area (maybe under the ^ overflow arrow).

Updating from an earlier version: install over it. Your crow, its trust and
its treasures are kept, and your level stays the same.

New in 1.4.0
------------
* 50 friendship levels. Levels 1 to 20 are exactly as before; levels 21 to 50
  come after them for the closest friends, with new milestones at 35 and 50.
* Droppings: now and then your crow leaves a white dropping on your screen.
  Press on it and rub it away with your pointer (or click it three times).
  Left there, it makes the crow unhappy and then unwell: it coughs, slows
  down and wears a fever ice bag with a thermometer in its beak until it gets
  better. It cannot die. Settings > Crow shows its health; Settings >
  Behaviour turns droppings off.

Using it
--------
Every time it starts, your crow waits in a cardboard box taped with red tape
and marked OPEN ME. Click the box and it pops out.

Everything else is in the tray menu (click the crow icon) and in the menu you
get by right-clicking the crow.
* Pick it up: press on the crow and drag, then let go anywhere.
* Pet it: stroke your pointer back and forth over it without clicking.
* Feed it: drag a snack to its beak, or use Feed in the tray menu.
* Clean up: rub its droppings away with your pointer.
* How it feels: now and then it tells you in a bubble (Hungry, Tired, Happy,
  Satisfied, Sad, Messy, Sick). Click it to ask. Settings > Crow shows it live.
* Music: when Spotify, Deezer, YouTube Music or a music video is playing, it
  sometimes dances. Turn it off in Settings > Behaviour.
* Treasures: click shiny things on your desktop to keep them. Once it trusts
  you, the crow brings you gifts too (Settings > Treasures).
* Friendship: Settings > Friendship shows your level (1 to 50), the five
  stages of trust and seventeen milestones to unlock.

Quit: tray menu (or right-click the crow) > Quit Desktop Crow.
Uninstall: Windows Settings > Apps > Desktop Crow > Uninstall.
Save file: %APPDATA%\desktop-crow\save.json
