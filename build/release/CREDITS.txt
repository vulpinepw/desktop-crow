Everything you see and hear from the crow is generated by the program itself:
the crow is a procedural 3D rig drawn at run time, its animations (dancing,
being petted and pooping included) are procedural, the snacks, shiny things,
gifts, the cardboard box, the mood bubbles, the droppings, the fever ice bag,
the thermometer and the sponge cursor are vector drawings in code, and the
sounds are synthesized at run time. The roses, brambles, blackberries and
falling petals of the Settings window are drawn and animated by code as well.
No third-party art, sprites, models or audio files are used.

Privacy
-------
Desktop Crow does not use the network. To dance to your music it asks the
operating system what is playing (the Windows media controls through a small
PowerShell helper, or MPRIS on Linux). Nothing is stored or sent anywhere, and
turning off "Dances to your music" in Settings stops the check.
